How School Day Status Works

Simple interpretation of completed daily gap captures.

Green = Reported: that school was not found in the pending-school list when the complete daily capture finished. Red = Not Reported: the school was explicitly found pending that day. The school table lists schools that had at least one pending day in the selected period.
